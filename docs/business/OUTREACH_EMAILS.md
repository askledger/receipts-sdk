connection if any]`, `[brief / one-pager link]`, and signoff. Keep
each email short — none should exceed 12 lines of body text.

## How to use these

- **Always attach the brief or one-pager**, even if the email is
  short. The reader will skim it, then decide whether to reply. The
  email is permission to attach; the brief does the explaining.
- **Subject lines avoid hype.** No "revolutionary," no "10x," no
  "killer." Calibrated executive recipients read these as noise and
  delete.
- **Two-week silence is a no.** Move on. Send one follow-up after 7
  days (template 6 above). After that, route effort to a different
  contact.
- **Never quote the price in the first email.** Plans are in the
  brief if they want them. Quoting prices unsolicited reads
  transactional.
- **Personalise the opening line.** One sentence referencing
  something specific to the recipient — a recent talk, a paper, a
  shared connection, a quote in their company's blog. Generic
  openings produce generic results.
