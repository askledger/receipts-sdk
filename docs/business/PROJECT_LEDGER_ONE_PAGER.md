# Project Ledger · One-page brief

### Open-source runtime trust substrate for enterprise AI

Every AI call inside a company becomes a cryptographically signed,
independently verifiable receipt — proof of what the model did, when,
under what policy, for any regulator, auditor, customer, or insurer.

---

**The category.** Cisco and Palo Alto have spent $200M+ in the last
twelve months acquiring AI gateway, model provenance, and AI security
positions. The **runtime accountability layer** — what the model
actually did at runtime, verifiable without the vendor — is the last
unclaimed space in the AI infrastructure stack. EU AI Act enforcement
in August 2026 turns the regulatory pressure binding. Munich Re,
Mosaic, and Armilla are already underwriting AI liability and need a
signed evidence feed.

**What is built.** Apache-2.0 open-source. Five language SDKs.
Specification published as PL-RFC-001 through PL-RFC-010 with a
three-level conformance program. Nine native integrations (Cursor,
Claude Code, LiteLLM, LangChain, Vercel AI SDK, AutoGen, Portkey,
Cloudflare Worker, Kong). Drop-in OpenAI-compatible proxy covering
the entire OpenAI-compatible IDE ecosystem in one install.
Admin console with role-specific dashboards for IT, Compliance, HR,
Legal, Finance, MRM, Insurance, and Data Lineage. Browser
extension with corporate-OIDC identity binding. HSM-backed signing
across AWS KMS, Azure Key Vault, GCP KMS, PKCS#11. Production-grade
hardening verified against 66 mandatory controls.

**Market.** TAM $5-15B by 2030. SAM $500M-2B today. Target SOM
$50-150M ARR within 3-5 years. Comparable open-core trajectories:
HashiCorp at $5.1B IPO, GitLab at $7B IPO, Snyk at $7.4B, Chainguard
at $40M ARR in 24 months.

**Stage.** Engineering complete. Publication, design-partner
outreach, and LF AI Sandbox submission in flight this month.
Pre-revenue with three design-partner conversations active across
BFSI, AI-vendor SaaS, and insurance-tech.

**Ask.** Expert review, design-partner pilots, standards-body
