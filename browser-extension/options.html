Project Ledger settings

Your receipts stay on your machine by default. Configure ship-to-corp-IT only with your CISO's blessing.

Local-only mode (default)

Every receipt is signed with a keypair generated in this browser. The private key never leaves your machine. Receipts are stored encrypted in chrome.storage.local.

Corporate ingest (optional)

Your corporate IT can configure an ingest URL. With your explicit consent, every signed receipt will be shipped to that URL in addition to local storage. The corporate side can never modify your local receipt history.

Only enable this if you trust your corporate IT environment. The receipts include hashes of your prompts — not the prompt text — but metadata (vendor, model, time, URL path) is shipped.

Your public key

Share this with anyone verifying your receipts. The private key stays in this browser.
